Returns & Exchanges

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Our 30-Day Guarantee

We stand behind every piece we create. If you're not completely satisfied, we offer free returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery — no questions asked. Your confidence in Ori9inalz is our priority.

Eligibility

To be eligible for a return or exchange, items must be:

  • Returned within 30 days of the delivery date
  • Unworn and in original condition
  • All original tags and packaging intact
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt)

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
  • Custom or personalized orders
  • Items showing signs of wear, alteration, or damage not caused by us

How to Start a Return

To initiate a return or exchange:

  • Email returns@ori9inalz.com with your order number and reason for return
  • We'll send you a prepaid return label within 24 hours
  • Pack your item securely and drop it off at any USPS location
  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of receiving your return

Exchanges

We offer size exchanges for the same product where inventory allows. Due to the limited-production nature of our products, exchanges for different colorways are subject to availability. If your desired item is out of stock, a full refund will be issued.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be credited to the original payment method within 3–5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, contact us immediately at support@ori9inalz.com with photos. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at our expense.

International Returns

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using a tracked shipping service. Ori9inalz is not responsible for returns lost in transit.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at returns@ori9inalz.com or via our Contact page. We typically respond within 4 business hours.

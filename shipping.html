Shipping Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 24 hours of placement on business days (Monday–Friday, excluding US federal holidays). Orders placed on weekends or holidays will ship the next business day. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order has shipped.

US Shipping

We offer free express shipping on all US orders with no minimum purchase required.

  • Express Shipping (Free): 2–4 business days — Continental US
  • Overnight Shipping: $35 — Next business day delivery (order by 12 PM EST)
  • Alaska & Hawaii: 4–7 business days (free)

International Shipping

We ship internationally to over 50 countries. International shipping rates and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on destination.

  • Canada: 5–8 business days — From $18
  • Europe: 7–14 business days — From $22
  • Rest of World: 10–20 business days — From $28

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or fees imposed by their country. Ori9inalz is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can track your package directly at Track My Order or via the carrier's website. If you haven't received tracking information within 48 hours, contact support@ori9inalz.com.

Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your order is lost or arrives damaged, contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date at support@ori9inalz.com. We will work with the carrier to resolve the issue and ensure you receive your order or a full refund.

Address Errors

Please ensure your shipping address is accurate at checkout. Ori9inalz is not responsible for orders shipped to incorrectly entered addresses. If you notice an error, contact us immediately at support@ori9inalz.com before your order ships.
